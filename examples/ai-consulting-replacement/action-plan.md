## Goal

Verify whether "AI will quickly replace traditional consulting" is a strategic signal or only a media narrative.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Find at least 3 independent cases of enterprise budget moving from consulting vendors to AI tooling.
- [ ] Identify which workflow is replaced: research, slide production, data analysis, implementation, or client communication.
- [ ] Separate productivity enhancement from vendor replacement.
- [ ] Decide whether the project should pivot, watch, or ignore.

## Actions

- Search for budget migration evidence.
- Search for customer replacement stories with names, dates, and purchase context.
- Compare AI tool adoption against consulting headcount or vendor spend changes.
- Record missing evidence explicitly.

## Stop Condition

Stop when there are either 3 concrete replacement cases or 3 review cycles with only media heat and no commercial replacement chain.

## Falsifying Signal

The hypothesis weakens if adoption stories show AI used inside existing consulting delivery rather than replacing vendors or budgets.
